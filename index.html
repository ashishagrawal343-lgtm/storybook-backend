Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free teaser preview work?
You enter your child's details, select a theme and language, and optionally upload a photo. In just ~40 seconds, our AI paints your child's personalized front cover and writes an opening lullaby rhyme for you to inspect for free.
What languages are supported?
TwinkleTale supports 11 languages: English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, and Portuguese! All stories and poems are composed in the authentic script of the selected language.
What is the difference between Treasury (12 pages) and Grand Treasury (24 pages)?
The Treasury Edition (₹199, original ₹499 • 60% OFF) has 12 interior pages structured as 6 spreads (6 full-page left illustrations + 6 right verse pages). The Grand Treasury (₹299, original ₹999 • 70% OFF) features 24 interior pages (12 full-page left illustrations + 12 right verse pages) with an expanded bedtime story.
Can I print the storybook?
Yes! Your storybook is compiled with high-resolution vector fonts and 300 DPI illustrations formatted to 600x800pt portrait. You can easily print it at home or at any digital printing shop.
