Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our personalized children's books.

How does the photo likeness work?
Our visual identity model analyzes key facial contours, eye shape, smile, hair color/texture, and distinctive features (like glasses or cultural headwear) from your uploaded photo. It then stylizes your child into a warm, painterly storybook illustration while preserving their genuine character.
How fast is delivery?
Your customized storybook PDF is generated automatically by our high-performance AI studio in under 3 to 5 minutes. A secure download link is sent directly to your email address, and you can also download it right on your confirmation screen.
Can I print the storybook?
Yes! Your storybook is delivered as a 300 DPI high-resolution PDF file. You can easily read it on an iPad, Kindle, or tablet, or print it at home or through services like Walgreens, Shutterfly, Staples, or FedEx Office.
